Robotics & machine vision

Part2FullNet: perception under occlusion

RoboMaster RMUL · Robot vision and embedded deployment

Visual target detection, tracking and spatial completion for sentry robots. My work connects geometric priors and temporal fusion to robust pose estimation under occlusion, with deployment optimization on Jetson Orin NX.

Robotic manipulation from simulation to reality

Tsinghua AIR summer program

A MuJoCo and ACT imitation-learning pipeline for AIRBOT Play, combining domain randomization and adaptive action chunks for transfer to real grasping tasks.

Embedded AI & hardware systems

An intelligent desktop-cleaning assistant

National Undergraduate Integrated Circuit Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition

A working perception–planning–execution system on a RISC-V platform: YOLOv5s detects objects, language-model planning translates instructions into actions, and a SCARA mechanism executes them. Quantization, pruning and pipelined preprocessing support EIC7700 NPU deployment.

National Second Prize and East China Regional First Prize, 2025.

Wearable sensing & entrepreneurship

Co-founder · AI development · Nanjing Qingning Sensing Technology

To make everyday walking and foot-pressure changes easier to understand, we developed a smart-insole prototype for ongoing movement assessment and personalized feedback.

As co-founder responsible for AI development, I worked across data collection and organization, analysis development, and integration with the wearable prototype. The project focuses on turning everyday movement records into useful information for personal well-being.
