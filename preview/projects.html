Robotics & machine vision

Part2FullNet: perception under occlusion

Third Place · RoboMaster RMUL 2026, Jiangsu Station
3V3 competition · Robot vision and embedded deployment

For RoboMaster 3V3 robot combat, I developed sentry vision to track moving armor plates despite occlusion and incomplete corners. Part2FullNet recovers spatial structure and 6D pose from partial RGB observations using geometric priors, depth calibration and EKF temporal fusion. Jetson Orin NX deployment with TensorRT INT8 and DLA achieved <25 ms end-to-end latency, >75% dynamic-target hit rate and <7.5 mm localization error in project evaluation.

Robotic manipulation from simulation to reality

Tsinghua AIR summer program

I built a Sim2Real manipulation pipeline for AIRBOT Play, connecting MuJoCo simulation, ACT imitation learning and real-robot grasping. Domain randomization and adaptive action chunks supported transfer from virtual tasks to physical execution.

Embedded AI & hardware systems

An intelligent desktop-cleaning assistant

National Second Prize, 2025
National Undergraduate Integrated Circuit Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition

A perception–planning–execution system on RISC-V: YOLOv5s detects tabletop objects, a DeepSeek-based agent interprets instructions and plans tidying tasks, and a SCARA arm executes them. I adapted the detector to the EIC7700 NPU through quantization, structured pruning and hardware-aware data layouts, overlapping preprocessing with inference. Project evaluation reached 97.4% mAP and 3 ms inference latency.

Wearable sensing & AI for healthcare

Co-founder · AI development
Nanjing Qingning Sensing Technology

To make everyday walking and foot-pressure changes easier to understand, we developed a smart-insole prototype for ongoing movement assessment and personalized feedback.

As co-founder responsible for AI development, I worked across data collection and organization, analysis development, and integration with the wearable prototype. The project focuses on turning everyday movement records into useful information for personal well-being.
