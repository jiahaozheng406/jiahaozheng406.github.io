Beyond research

Curiosity does not stop at the lab. Football, running and time outdoors bring a different rhythm to my days.

Football & inspiration

Kylian Mbappé is one of the athletes I admire. His pace, confidence and willingness to take responsibility in difficult moments resonate with me. In research, I value a similar willingness to take on hard problems and keep learning when a first attempt fails.

Football is also about teamwork: reading the game, adapting quickly, and trusting others. These are qualities I appreciate both on the pitch and in collaborative research.

Running & the outdoors

I enjoy marathon running and hiking: sustained effort, a clear head, and a reminder that progress usually comes one step at a time.

Community

At NJUPT, I helped organize innovation and entrepreneurship activities connecting students, alumni and industry. I also participated in the National Youth Science Camp, sharing the enjoyment of science with younger students.

Why “++”?

A small nod to my name and to the increment operator: stay positive, learn something new, and improve a little each day.
