About me

I am an undergraduate in Internet of Things Engineering at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT). My work explores how learning-based systems can reconstruct, understand and interact with the 3D world.

I have been pre-admitted to the master's program in Artificial Intelligence at the State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, through recommendation with entrance-examination exemption. I expect to begin in September 2027.

During my undergraduate studies, I have worked on sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting, executable CAD generation, and Real2Sim environments for robot navigation. Earlier work in physics-aware sensing shaped my interest in combining learned representations with structural and physical constraints.

Selected research

TopoGen: Topology-Guided Multimodal Generation for Executable CadQuery Synthesis

ICLR 2027 submission · Second author

Topology-guided CAD programs from point clouds and multi-view images. Constraint graphs and BRep-derived supervision connect visual observations to executable modeling operations. Topology Relative Policy Optimization (ToRPO) uses essential-operation matching for structural feedback, while TopoCAD-1M augments training data with topology constraints.

KV Thickets: Task Experts from Random Prefix-Cache Perturbations

ICLR 2027 submission · Co-author

Reusable task adaptation through random prefix-KV perturbations, keeping model weights and prefix text fixed. Candidates are selected on labeled examples and reused across inputs, with batched evaluation under shared weights in vLLM. The study spans seven models and seven benchmarks; its evaluation is descriptive because benchmark pools had been examined previously.

Towards Robust Sparse-View 3D Gaussian Splatting via Hierarchical Depth and Multi-View Consistency

ACM ICMR 2026 · Second author

Monocular-depth alignment with sparse SfM geometry, semantic depth supervision and multi-view consistency improve reconstruction from sparse observations. My work covered depth alignment, hierarchical supervision and evaluation, addressing geometric drift and overfitting when only a few views are available.

Real2Sim for embodied navigation

Research at ICT-CAS IIP-VIPL · 2025–2026

Dynamic filtering, VGGT initialization and globally aligned 3DGS turn long road videos into editable scenes. Mesh conversion and asset editing connect reconstruction to Isaac Sim, supporting teacher–student navigation transfer and quadruped deployment. The focus is spatial consistency and a usable bridge between real observations and robot simulation.

Diamond Thermometry Beyond the Zero-Phonon Line via Physics-Aware Spectral Trend Learning

Applied Physics Letters, 2026 · First author

Physics-aware spectral learning for robust NV-center thermometry when conventional zero-phonon-line features become unreliable. I designed a physics-constrained ResNet to learn phonon-sideband evolution and evaluated cross-sample adaptation, connecting spectral structure with temperature inference.

Real-Time On-Chip Nanosecond Pulse Capture with NV Centers: U-Net Denoising and Signal Reconstruction

Chinese Physics B · Accepted manuscript, 2026 · Second author

Physics-aware U-Net denoising and signal inversion recover weak transient signals in NV-center sensing. My contribution focused on the loss design and reconstruction pipeline, combining physical constraints with learned denoising under low signal-to-noise conditions.

Projects & community

Part2FullNet: perception under occlusion

Lightweight spatial completion and 6D pose estimation for RoboMaster robots, combining geometric priors, depth calibration and EKF temporal fusion. Deployment on Jetson Orin NX uses TensorRT INT8 and DLA execution for real-time perception.

Robotic manipulation from simulation to reality

At the Tsinghua AIR summer program, I worked on a MuJoCo and ACT imitation-learning pipeline for AIRBOT Play. Domain randomization and adaptive action chunks support policy transfer from simulation to real grasping tasks.

An intelligent desktop assistant

A RISC-V system connecting YOLOv5s perception, DeepSeek-based task planning and SCARA execution. Model quantization, structured pruning and pipelined preprocessing support deployment on the EIC7700 NPU.

Wearable sensing & entrepreneurship

As co-founder of Nanjing Qingning Sensing Technology, I worked on the AI side of wearable sensing: multimodal data alignment, automated database construction, temporal gait modeling and personalized analysis. The work explores how plantar-pressure and motion signals can support continuous assessment.

I also helped organize innovation and entrepreneurship talks at NJUPT, connecting students, alumni and industry. Outside research, I enjoy football, marathon running and climbing.
