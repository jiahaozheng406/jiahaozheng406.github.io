About me

I am an undergraduate in Internet of Things Engineering at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT), where I rank first in my major. My work explores how learning-based systems can reconstruct, understand and interact with the 3D world.

I have been pre-admitted to the master's program in Artificial Intelligence at the State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, through recommendation with entrance-examination exemption. I expect to begin in September 2027.

During my undergraduate studies, I have worked on sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting, executable CAD generation, and Real2Sim environments for robot navigation. Earlier work in physics-aware sensing shaped my interest in combining learned representations with structural and physical constraints.

Selected research

TopoGen: Topology-Guided Multimodal Generation for Executable CadQuery Synthesis

ICLR 2027 submission · Second author

Topology-guided CAD programs from point clouds and multi-view images. Constraint graphs, BRep supervision and ToRPO connect visual geometry to executable modeling operations.

KV Thickets: Task Experts from Random Prefix-Cache Perturbations

ICLR 2027 submission · Co-author

Reusable task adaptation through random prefix-KV perturbations, keeping model weights and prefix text fixed. Batched candidate evaluation uses shared weights in vLLM.

Towards Robust Sparse-View 3D Gaussian Splatting via Hierarchical Depth and Multi-View Consistency

ACM ICMR 2026 · Second author

Monocular-depth alignment, semantic depth supervision and multi-view consistency improve reconstruction from sparse observations.

PaperCode

Real2Sim for embodied navigation

Research at ICT-CAS IIP-VIPL · 2025–2026

Dynamic filtering, VGGT initialization and globally aligned 3DGS turn long road videos into editable Isaac Sim environments for teacher–student navigation transfer.

Diamond Thermometry Beyond the Zero-Phonon Line via Physics-Aware Spectral Trend Learning

Applied Physics Letters, 2026 · First author

Physics-aware spectral learning for robust NV-center thermometry when conventional zero-phonon-line features become unreliable.

Real-Time On-Chip Nanosecond Pulse Capture with NV Centers: U-Net Denoising and Signal Reconstruction

Chinese Physics B, 2026 · Third author

Physics-aware U-Net denoising and signal inversion recover weak transient signals in NV-center sensing.
