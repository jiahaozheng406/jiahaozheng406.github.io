About me

I am an undergraduate in Internet of Things Engineering at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT). My work explores how learning-based systems can reconstruct, understand and interact with the 3D world.

I have been pre-admitted to the master's program in Artificial Intelligence at the State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems (MAIS), Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), through recommendation with entrance-examination exemption. I expect to begin in September 2027.

My undergraduate research spans 3D vision, robotics, embodied intelligence and computer graphics, building on earlier work in physical sensing that combined representation learning with structural and physical constraints.

I also co-founded a wearable-sensing venture, focusing on AI development, and am currently learning about AI infrastructure and computer architecture.

Also known as ++, the incremental optimist: curious about new ideas, and always learning a little more.

Undergraduate research journey

  1. Physics-aware AI & scientific sensing

    NV-center quantum sensing, spectral learning and signal reconstruction, connecting physical knowledge with data-driven models.

  2. Robotic vision & embedded intelligence

    Visual perception, target tracking and spatial reasoning for robots, with deployment on embedded processors and hardware-aware optimization.

  3. 3D vision, graphics & embodied AI

    Sparse-view 3D reconstruction, multimodal CAD generation and Real2Sim–Sim2Real, toward editable worlds that robots can perceive and act in.
