Selected research

TopoGen: Topology-Guided Multimodal Generation for Executable CadQuery Synthesis

ICLR 2027 submission · Second author

Topology-guided CAD programs from point clouds and multi-view images. Constraint graphs and BRep-derived supervision connect visual observations to executable modeling operations. Topology Relative Policy Optimization (ToRPO) uses essential-operation matching for structural feedback, while TopoCAD-1M augments training data with topology constraints.

KV Thickets: Task Experts from Random Prefix-Cache Perturbations

ICLR 2027 submission · Co-author

Reusable task adaptation through random prefix-KV perturbations, keeping model weights and prefix text fixed. Candidates are selected on labeled examples and reused across inputs, with batched evaluation under shared weights in vLLM. The study spans seven models and seven benchmarks; its evaluation is descriptive because benchmark pools had been examined previously.

Towards Robust Sparse-View 3D Gaussian Splatting via Hierarchical Depth and Multi-View Consistency

ACM ICMR 2026 · Second author

Monocular-depth alignment with sparse SfM geometry, semantic depth supervision and multi-view consistency improve reconstruction from sparse observations. My work covered depth alignment, hierarchical supervision and evaluation, addressing geometric drift and overfitting when only a few views are available.

Real2Sim–Sim2Real: an end-to-end embodied navigation pipeline

Research at ICT-CAS IIP-VIPL · 2025–2026

An end-to-end workflow from real-world observations to reconstructed, editable simulation environments and back to physical robots. Dynamic filtering and globally aligned 3DGS support scene reconstruction; Isaac Sim connects scene assets to policy learning, followed by teacher–student transfer and RGB-D quadruped navigation in the real world.

Diamond Thermometry Beyond the Zero-Phonon Line via Physics-Aware Spectral Trend Learning

Applied Physics Letters, 2026 · First author

Physics-aware spectral learning for robust NV-center thermometry when conventional zero-phonon-line features become unreliable. I designed a physics-constrained ResNet to learn phonon-sideband evolution and evaluated cross-sample adaptation, connecting spectral structure with temperature inference.

Real-Time On-Chip Nanosecond Pulse Capture with NV Centers: U-Net Denoising and Signal Reconstruction

Chinese Physics B · 2026 · Co-author

An AI-assisted pulse-recovery pipeline pairs U-Net denoising with physical constraints to recover weak NV-center signals. My work focused on physics-aware loss design and digital signal inversion, evaluated on 40,000 pulse pairs. Gradient-driven state latching and two-stage timing compensation turn denoised analog traces into digital sequences, preserving pulse transitions rather than merely smoothing noise. The pipeline achieved 21.39–29.57 dB SNR improvement, linking learned denoising to on-chip waveform and sequence reconstruction.

代表性研究

TopoGen：面向可执行 CadQuery 程序的拓扑引导多模态生成

ICLR 2027 投稿，第二作者。从点云和多视角图像生成 CAD 程序。约束图与来自边界表示（BRep）的监督把视觉观测与可执行建模操作联系起来。ToRPO 以关键操作匹配提供结构反馈，TopoCAD-1M 则为训练数据补充拓扑约束。

KV Thickets：利用随机前缀缓存扰动构建任务专家

ICLR 2027 投稿，共同作者。保持模型权重和前缀文本固定，对前缀 KV 缓存施加随机扰动，在带标签样例上选出可跨输入复用的候选，并在 vLLM 上以共享模型权重进行批量评估。研究涉及七个模型和七个基准。由于此前已查看过基准样本池，结果按描述性证据呈现。

稀疏视角三维高斯溅射

ACM ICMR 2026，第二作者。将单目深度与稀疏 SfM 几何对齐，并结合分层深度监督和多视角一致性，缓解少量观测下的几何漂移和过拟合。我参与深度对齐、分层监督与评估。

Real2Sim 与 Sim2Real：端到端具身导航

中科院计算所 IIP–VIPL，2025–2026。从真实观测构建可编辑仿真环境，再将策略部署到物理机器人。流程结合动态目标过滤、全局对齐的 3DGS 和 Isaac Sim，并通过教师—学生迁移支持四足机器人的 RGB-D 导航。

基于物理约束光谱趋势学习的金刚石测温

Applied Physics Letters，2026，第一作者。面向传统零声子线特征不稳定的 NV 色心测温场景，设计带物理约束的 ResNet 学习声子边带演化，并评估跨样本适配。

NV 色心片上纳秒脉冲捕获

Chinese Physics B，2026，共同作者。将 U-Net 降噪与物理约束结合，从微弱信号恢复脉冲。我负责物理约束损失设计与数字信号反演。流程使用 40,000 对脉冲进行评估，报告的信噪比提升为 21.39–29.57 dB。
