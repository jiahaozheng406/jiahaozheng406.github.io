Open source

LMMs-Eval: trustworthy multimodal evaluation

Open-source Contributor · Oct 2026 · Project: ~4.5k GitHub stars

LMMs-Eval provides unified evaluation infrastructure for multimodal model development, supporting 100+ image, video, audio and text tasks. To address a community-reported mismatch between AI2D questions and diagram variants, I audited all 3,088 questions and added per-question image selection, with pinned data, checksum validation, regression tests and real-model pipeline checks. The new task pairs each question with its intended diagram, making its inputs reproducible while keeping the legacy task available for comparison.

Robotics & machine vision

EVA: Embodied Vision-Language-Action Robot System

Collaborative open-source robotics project with Liyao Wu

EVA is an integrated six-DoF robot operation system that brings camera perception, model inference and motion control into one workflow. A browser-based interface provides 3D robot visualization, camera viewing and interactive control, while Python coordinates devices and perception and a C++ kinematics layer drives robot execution. This connects visual feedback with robot operation in a single software stack, with support for edge-oriented perception deployment.

Part2FullNet: perception under occlusion

Third Place · RoboMaster RMUL 2026, Jiangsu Station
3V3 competition · Robot vision and embedded deployment

For RoboMaster 3V3 robot combat, I developed sentry vision to track moving armor plates despite occlusion and incomplete corners. Part2FullNet recovers spatial structure and 6D pose from partial RGB observations using geometric priors, depth calibration and EKF temporal fusion. Jetson Orin NX deployment with TensorRT INT8 and DLA achieved <25 ms end-to-end latency, >75% dynamic-target hit rate and <7.5 mm localization error in project evaluation.

Robotic manipulation from simulation to reality

Tsinghua AIR summer program

To bridge the gap between simulated manipulation and physical grasping, I built a Sim2Real pipeline for AIRBOT Play at the Tsinghua AIR summer program. My work connected MuJoCo task development, ACT imitation learning, policy evaluation and real-robot deployment. Domain randomization and adaptive action chunks supported transfer across simulation and reality, bringing perception and grasp execution into a closed loop. Real-robot tests achieved >90% grasp success and 50% single-trial dual-target grasping success.

Embedded AI & hardware systems

An intelligent desktop-cleaning assistant

National Second Prize, 2025
National Undergraduate Integrated Circuit Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition

A perception–planning–execution system on RISC-V: YOLOv5s detects tabletop objects, a DeepSeek-based agent interprets instructions and plans tidying tasks, and a SCARA arm executes them. I adapted the detector to the EIC7700 NPU through quantization, structured pruning and hardware-aware data layouts, overlapping preprocessing with inference. Project evaluation reached 97.4% mAP and 3 ms inference latency, with a 50× end-to-end pipeline speedup.

Entrepreneurship: AI-powered wearable healthcare

Co-founder · AI development
Nanjing Qingning Sensing Technology (Seed round completed; valuation exceeding RMB 30 million)

Our venture focuses on research into AI-assisted early screening for diabetes using wearable plantar-pressure and gait data. We developed a smart-insole prototype to collect everyday foot-loading and movement signals and explore whether their patterns can support screening. As co-founder responsible for AI development, I worked on data collection and organization, AI-based pressure and gait analysis, and integration with the wearable prototype. The project aims to provide accessible, personalized screening support; its diagnostic accuracy and clinical utility remain to be validated.

机器人、机器视觉与嵌入式系统

  • EVA 六自由度机器人系统：与 Liyao Wu 合作，将相机感知、模型推理与运动控制整合在同一套系统中。浏览器端支持三维可视化和交互控制，Python 协调感知及设备，C++ 运动学层驱动真机执行。

  • 清华 AIR 机械臂 Sim2Real：串联 MuJoCo 任务开发、ACT 模仿学习和真机部署，采用域随机化及自适应动作分块。真机测试中抓取成功率超过 90%。
